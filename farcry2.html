ABOUT FAR CRY 2

Far Cry 2 takes place in a fictional African conflict zone where players take the role of a mercenary searching for the Jackal.

The game focuses on open-world exploration, missions, combat, vehicles and survival. Players can travel through a large environment while completing objectives for different factions.

The dynamic world features changing weather, day and night cycles and environmental hazards, giving the game a distinctive open-world experience.

GAME FEATURES

  • Large open-world African environment
  • First-person combat and exploration
  • Multiple weapons and equipment
  • Vehicles for exploring the world
  • Dynamic weather system
  • Day and night cycle
  • Story missions and side activities
