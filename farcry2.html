About Far Cry 2

Far Cry 2 puts you in the role of a mercenary operating in a fictional African conflict. Your missions can take you across deserts, savannas, villages and other dangerous areas.

The game features an open-world environment where you can approach missions in different ways. You can travel through the world, work with different factions and use a variety of weapons and vehicles.

Dynamic environmental systems, changing weather, fire propagation and non-scripted enemy behavior contribute to the game's survival-focused atmosphere.
