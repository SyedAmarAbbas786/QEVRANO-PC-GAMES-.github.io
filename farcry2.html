About Far Cry 2

Far Cry 2 is an open-world first-person shooter developed by Ubisoft Montreal and released in 2008.

The game takes place in a fictional African country experiencing a civil war. You play as a mercenary who is hired to track down an arms dealer known as The Jackal.

The large open world allows players to choose different routes and approaches to missions. The environment, weather, fire and enemy behavior are important parts of the gameplay experience.

Story

A civil war has divided the country between competing factions. You arrive as a mercenary with one major objective: locate and eliminate The Jackal, an arms dealer supplying weapons to both sides.

As the conflict develops, you become involved with different factions, missions and alliances while attempting to survive the hostile environment.
