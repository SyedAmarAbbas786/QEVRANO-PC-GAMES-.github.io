About Game

Far Cry 2 is an open-world first-person shooter set in an African environment. Players take on the role of a mercenary sent into a conflict between rival factions.

The game features a large explorable world with vehicles, weapons, dynamic weather, stealth options and missions that can be approached in different ways.

Features

🌍 Large open-world African environment
🔫 Wide selection of weapons
🚙 Drive vehicles across the map
🔥 Dynamic fire and environmental effects
🎯 Flexible mission approaches
🗺️ Explore villages, deserts and wilderness
