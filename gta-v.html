About Grand Theft Auto V

Grand Theft Auto V takes place in the fictional state of San Andreas, featuring the city of Los Santos and the surrounding Blaine County. The story follows three protagonists whose lives become connected through a series of events and heists. The game features a large open world, vehicles, missions, exploration and GTA Online.

System Requirements

GTAV Legacy — Minimum

OS: Windows 10

Graphics: NVIDIA 9800 GT 1GB / AMD HD 4870 1GB

OS: Windows 10
Processor: Intel Core i5 3470 @ 3.2GHz / AMD X8 FX-8350 @ 4GHz

Storage: 125 GB
DirectX: DirectX 11


Note: Rockstar currently lists separate system requirements for GTAV Enhanced and GTAV Legacy. GTAV Enhanced requires Windows 10, 8 GB RAM, a supported dedicated GPU and 105 GB of SSD storage at minimum. The Legacy version has lower minimum requirements.
