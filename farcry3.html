About Game

Far Cry 3 is an open-world first-person shooter set on the tropical Rook Islands. Players take control of Jason Brody, who becomes stranded on the islands and must survive while searching for his friends.

The game combines exploration, shooting, stealth, hunting and character progression. Players can explore the island, capture outposts, complete missions and discover many hidden areas.
