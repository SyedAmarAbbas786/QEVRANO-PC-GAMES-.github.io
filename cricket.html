ABOUT GAME

Don Bradman Cricket is a cricket simulation developed by Big Ant Studios. The game focuses on realistic cricket gameplay and gives players control over batting, bowling and fielding.


Players can take part in different match formats, create and customize players and teams, and compete through cricket-focused game modes.

FEATURES

Realistic batting mechanics
Bowling and fielding controls
Multiple cricket match formats
Player and team customization
Career-focused gameplay
Detailed cricket stadiums
