About GTA VI

GTA VI takes players back to Vice City in the biggest and most immersive Grand Theft Auto world yet.

The story follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos as they become caught up in a dangerous journey across the fictional state of Leonida.

Explore cities, highways, beaches, countryside and many other locations while experiencing Rockstar Games' next-generation open-world adventure.
