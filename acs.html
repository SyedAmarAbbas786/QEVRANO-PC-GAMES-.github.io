ABOUT THE GAME

Assassin's Creed Syndicate is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Ubisoft Quebec and published by Ubisoft. Set in London in 1868, the game follows twin Assassins Jacob Frye and Evie Frye as they work to free the city from corrupt forces during the Industrial Revolution.
