ABOUT GAME

Assassin's Creed II is an action-adventure game set during the Italian Renaissance. Players take control of Ezio Auditore and explore locations including Florence, Venice and surrounding regions.


The game combines exploration, parkour, stealth, combat and story missions while allowing players to discover the world and interact with different environments.
