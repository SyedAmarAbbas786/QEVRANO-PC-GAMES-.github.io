About Game

Assassin's Creed II is an action-adventure game set during the Italian Renaissance. You play as Ezio Auditore, a young nobleman whose life changes after a personal tragedy. His journey takes him across cities such as Florence, Venice and Monteriggioni while he learns the skills of an assassin and becomes involved in a larger conflict.

The game combines exploration, stealth, combat, parkour and story-driven missions. Players can explore cities, climb buildings, interact with characters and discover hidden locations throughout Renaissance Italy.
