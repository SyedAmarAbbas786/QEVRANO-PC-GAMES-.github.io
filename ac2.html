About Game

Assassin's Creed II follows Ezio Auditore da Firenze, a young Italian noble who becomes an Assassin during the Renaissance.

Explore historic locations including Florence and Venice, climb buildings, move through crowded streets and uncover a story of family, vengeance and conspiracy.

The game expands on the original Assassin's Creed with a larger world, new abilities, equipment, characters and a deeper progression system.
