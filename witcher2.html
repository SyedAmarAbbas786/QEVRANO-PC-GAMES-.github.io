The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings is an action role-playing game developed by CD PROJEKT RED. Players take control of Geralt of Rivia, a monster hunter known as a witcher.


The adventure takes place in a politically unstable world where Geralt's decisions can significantly affect the story. The game features exploration, combat, character development, dialogue choices and multiple story paths.
