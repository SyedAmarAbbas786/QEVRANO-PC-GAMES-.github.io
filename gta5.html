About the Game

Grand Theft Auto V is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games.

The story follows Michael De Santa, Franklin Clinton and Trevor Philips as their lives become increasingly connected through criminal schemes and major heists.

Explore Los Santos and Blaine County, complete missions, discover activities and experience the single-player Story Mode.

OPEN WORLD Explore the huge city of Los Santos and surrounding Blaine County countryside.
THREE PROTAGONISTS Switch between Michael, Franklin and Trevor throughout the single-player story.
STORY MODE Experience a large cinematic campaign filled with missions, characters and heists.
VEHICLES Cars, motorcycles, aircraft and many other vehicles.
DETAILED WORLD Beaches, mountains, highways, neighborhoods and countless locations to discover.
PC VERSIONS PC players can access both GTAV Legacy and GTAV Enhanced.
