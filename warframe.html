About Warframe

Warframe is a free-to-play third-person action game developed and published by Digital Extremes. Players take the role of the Tenno and explore the Origin System through missions, quests and different combat activities.

The game focuses on fast movement, close and ranged combat, character customization and collecting equipment. Players can choose from a large selection of Warframes, each offering different abilities and play styles.

Warframe also features cooperative multiplayer and cross-platform play, allowing players on supported platforms to join together.
